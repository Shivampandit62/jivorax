About Jivora

Why Jivora? Useful tools. Made simple.

Because a percentage, a QR code or a word count should take seconds — not a signup, an upload and three cookie banners.

Jivora is a small collection of everyday tools that live in your browser. Calculators, formatters, generators and counters — each one does a single job well, loads instantly and asks nothing of you.

The problem with most online tools

Search for almost any simple tool and you land on a page wrapped in advertising, a "create a free account to see your result" wall, or a form that quietly ships your data to a server you've never heard of. The tool itself is usually an afterthought.

That's backwards. The tool should be the whole point: open the page, get the answer, get on with your day.

What Jivora believes

  • No accounts, ever. There is no login or signup anywhere on Jivora, and there never will be. You don't need to identify yourself to calculate a percentage.
  • Processing belongs on your device. Wherever possible, the work happens in your browser with JavaScript — your numbers, text, JSON and images are never uploaded. You can disconnect from the internet mid-task and most tools will keep working.
  • Free means free. Every tool is free to use, with no paywalled "premium" results or daily limits.
  • Honest roadmaps. Tools that aren't finished are labelled Coming soon instead of pretending to work. If a button exists, it works.
  • Speed is a feature. The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no framework and no build step, so pages load fast on any connection.
The short version: if a tool can run entirely in your browser, Jivora runs it there. Your data stays yours — see the Privacy Policy for the details.

How the tools are built

Every tool is hand-written JavaScript with no server-side component: the QR generator implements the QR standard directly, the image compressor re-encodes files on an off-screen canvas, and the calculators do their math locally. Nothing you type is transmitted by Jivora itself.

The site works from any static host — GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages — which keeps it fast, cheap to run and independent of any backend that could log your input.

What's next

The directory already lists the roadmap: more student and finance calculators, developer utilities and image tools are on the way. Each one will appear the moment it actually works — clearly marked, tested and just as private as everything else here.

Ideas, bugs or a tool you wish existed? Send a note — it all goes to a human.

Quick questions

The things people usually ask first.

Is Jivora really free?

Yes — every tool, with no accounts, limits or paywalled results. The site has no costs to pass on: it's static files served from a free host, and everything runs on your device.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no login or signup anywhere on Jivora, and none is planned. Open a tool and use it.

What data does Jivora collect?

Tool inputs — your numbers, text, JSON and images — are processed in your browser and never sent anywhere. Standard anonymous analytics may record page views and tool names, but not what you type. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I suggest a tool?

Absolutely. Use the contact page — suggestions from real users shape the roadmap directly.
