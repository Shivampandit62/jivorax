How to use this text cleaner

  1. Paste your text into the box — copied from emails, PDFs, websites or chat, however messy it is.
  2. Pick the fixes you want. All four are on by default; switch any of them off if you need that formatting kept.
  3. Check the output on the right — it updates with every keystroke, and the status line shows whether anything changed.
  4. Copy the result with one button, or press Clear to start over with a fresh paste.

Useful information

Pasted text is rarely clean. Word processors and websites hide characters you can't see — double spaces after sentences, tabs, non-breaking spaces, curly quotes — and those invisible extras break code, forms and spreadsheets later.

What each fix does

Collapse extra spaces turns runs of spaces and tabs between words into a single space. Trim every line removes leading and trailing whitespace, including the trailing spaces that sneak in at line ends. Remove blank-line runs keeps paragraph breaks but collapses three or more blank lines down to one. Straighten smart punctuation converts curly quotes and ellipses (…) into plain ASCII equivalents.

What it deliberately doesn't do

No words are rewritten, no lines are joined or split, and single line breaks are preserved exactly. This is whitespace and punctuation hygiene — not a rewriter. If you also need repeated lines removed, that's the dedicated Duplicate Line Remover.

Cleaning before you paste

Run text through this tool before pasting into a code editor, CSV, form field or database import. It is the fastest way to kill the "works in the preview but fails on submit" class of bugs caused by invisible characters.

Privacy: cleaning happens entirely in this tab. Your text is never transmitted, stored or logged.

Frequently asked questions

What does this tool clean up?

Double spaces, tabs between words, leading and trailing whitespace on every line, runs of blank lines, and smart (curly) quotes and ellipses that cause problems in code and forms. Each fix is a separate toggle, so you only apply the ones you want.

Will it change my wording or line breaks?

No. The cleaner never adds, removes or reorders words, and single line breaks stay exactly where they are — only whitespace and punctuation characters are normalised.

Why do smart quotes cause problems?

Curly quotes (“ ” ‘ ’) are multi-byte characters that many forms, spreadsheets and code files don't expect. If a pasted value refuses to match or compile, straightening the punctuation is often the fix.

Does it remove duplicate lines too?

No — that is a separate job. Use the Duplicate Line Remover when you also need repeated lines removed; this tool only fixes spacing and punctuation.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Cleaning runs entirely in your browser as you type — your text is never sent to a server or saved by Jivora.
